# Zapbar: Play Store release spec (Plan 5)

**Status:** design approved in conversation 2026-10-17; written for review.
**Parent spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-16-zapbar-design.md` §7 "Distribution". This spec replaces §7's iOS gate and refines the tip jar.
**Previous plans:** 1 heos-core, 2 app, 3 visual design, 4 quick access (all merged; app at 0.3.5).

## 1. Intent

Zapbar goes live on Google Play under the Code 418 organisation account as `app.zapbar.remote`. It is free, with a tip jar that unlocks cosmetic and silly perks only. zapbar.app hosts a landing page, the privacy policy and support. The owner, and then their partner, installs from Play; strangers can find and install it.

**Success criteria:**
- 1.0.0 is in production on Play, installed from the store by the owner and their partner.
- A license-tester purchase unlocks the perks and survives an uninstall/reinstall.
- zapbar.app serves `/`, `/privacy` and `/support` over HTTPS through the estate's standard path.
- Every claim on `/privacy` is backed by a test that fails if the app breaks it.
- The launch kit is ready, and nothing is posted without the owner's go.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-17):**
- Tip jar ships in v1 and pays out to the Individual payments profile (the known trap: payments hang off profile 0942-8785-7847, not the org).
- Rollout: internal track → production. No public beta.
- Build: local Gradle + ported sonora scripts (no EAS). Site: static nginx container on .210 behind nginx-pm + the Cloudflare tunnel.
- iOS gate: aim for iOS once Zapbar has **500+ installs or positive ratings coming in**. Builds will run on the owner's Mac mini; an Apple developer account still has to be registered. This replaces the parent spec's ~1k installs / ≥4.5★ gate.

## 2. Release build and signing

**Upload key:**
- `zapbar-upload.jks`, alias `zapbar`, generated once.
- Passwords go in the vault under scope `zapbar`; commit and push the vault after the set.
- The keystore is copied to `/mnt/e/projects/keystores/`, `/mnt/c/` (same layout as sonora), `.210:~/keystores/`, and is covered by restic.
- Play App Signing holds the real app key.

**Config plugin:** `android/` is generated by prebuild and gitignored, so release signing is wired in through a local config plugin (`apps/remote/plugins/with-release-signing.js`). It reads `ZAPBAR_UPLOAD_STORE_FILE`, `ZAPBAR_UPLOAD_STORE_PASSWORD`, `ZAPBAR_UPLOAD_KEY_PASSWORD` from the environment. A `bundleRelease` without them **fails**; it never falls back to the debug key. APK test builds keep working with an explicit `-PzapbarDebugSigned=true`.

**Permissions:**
- `app.json` `android.blockedPermissions` removes `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` and `VIBRATE`.
- The allowed list is exactly:
  - `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`, `CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE`;
  - `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`;
  - `com.android.vending.BILLING`;
  - plus permissions the toolchain adds that carry no user data. The plan enumerates them from the first AAB and rules on each.

**Versioning:** `scripts/version.mjs <semver>` sets `expo.version` and `expo.android.versionCode` together. versionCode = major·10000 + minor·100 + patch, which starts at 10000 for 1.0.0 and always increases.

**Release check:** `scripts/release-check.mjs <aab>` fails unless all of these hold:
- the signer certificate SHA-256 equals the upload key's (via `keytool -printcert -jarfile`);
- targetSdk = 36;
- the merged permissions equal the allowed list;
- versionCode is above the highest on any Play track (read through the API once the app exists).

**Publishing:**
- The sonora scripts are ported into `scripts/play/`:
  - `publish-internal.sh` uploads the AAB to the internal track;
  - `play-track-status.py` reads the track back, because the build log is never trusted;
  - `play-api-check.py` checks API access;
  - `promote.sh` promotes to production at 100%.
- Service account: `<service-account>` (vault `PLAY_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`, scope <vault-scope>).

**Manual, once (the owner, in the Console):**
1. Create the app.
2. Confirm free.
3. Upload the first AAB to internal.
4. Grant the service account access to Zapbar.
5. Add license testers.

`docs/release/first-upload.md` gives the click-by-click steps, including where the 12-testers/14-days rule shows (expected exempt for an organisation account; verify).

## 3. Play listing and declarations

**Text:**
- **Title:** "Zapbar: Remote for HEOS" (23 characters; Play's limit is 30).
- **Short description:** "The remote HEOS forgot to build. Designed for function, no bloat." (65 characters).
- **Full description:** features, requirements (a HEOS device on the same Wi-Fi), tested on Denon DHT-S716H, privacy summary. It ends with "Not affiliated with Denon or HEOS; trademarks belong to their owners."
- All of it is kept in `docs/release/listing.md`.

**Classification:**
- Category: Music & Audio.
- Content rating: Everyone (IARC questionnaire).
- Target audience: 13+ (not Families).
- No ads, no login.

**Graphics:**
- Six phone screenshots from the emulator against the real bar, in light and dark: remote, EQ, inputs, widgets, notification and slider. The room name is set as an app-side nickname, never written to the bar.
- Each gets a one-line caption band, rendered with Playwright.
- Feature graphic 1024×500 and icon 512×512 from `scripts/brand-assets.mjs`.
- Sources live in `docs/release/graphics/`.

**Foreground-service declaration (connectedDevice):**
- Use case: "controls the user's own soundbar on the local network from volume keys and a media notification while it plays".
- Video: recorded with `adb screenrecord` on the emulator, showing the notification and volume keys changing the bar. The owner uploads it to YouTube as unlisted.

**Data Safety:** "No data collected or shared". Before submission, the plan checks Google's Data Safety guidance on purchases processed by Google Play Billing, and adjusts if they must be declared.

**App access note for reviewers:** the app needs a HEOS device on the same network, with a link to the same video. No demo mode. If review rejects the app for this, a built-in demo soundbar becomes the fix in 1.0.1.

**Contact:** `support@zapbar.app` through Cloudflare Email Routing to the owner's mailbox. Enabling routing needs a token with Zone Settings:Edit.

## 4. zapbar.app

`apps/site`: three static HTML pages with no JavaScript, no cookies and no analytics.
- `/`: hero, tagline, Play badge, three screenshots, feature list.
- `/privacy`: no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads. The app talks only to devices on your local network; purchases are handled by Google Play. Settings stay on the phone. Contact address. Effective date.
- `/support`: requirements, tested hardware, FAQ (not found on Wi-Fi, volume keys on Samsung battery settings, restoring a tip), contact address.

**Brand:** forest #17301B / lime #C8F552, light and dark, Plus Jakarta Sans served from the site itself (no Google Fonts request).

**Hosting:**
- Scaffold with `~/projects/new.sh` for a static nginx container on .210, with the port from `claudify-port` and `restart: unless-stopped`.
- The image builds on .210.
- Published at `zapbar.app` (and `www` → apex) through nginx-pm and the Cloudflare tunnel.
- **Ask before going live** (outward-facing).

**Drift guard (published-claims rule):** `apps/remote/__tests__/privacy-claims.test.ts` pairs each negative claim on `/privacy` with a code predicate.
1. **Network tripwire (built here; the parent spec named it but it never existed):** every URL or host literal in `apps/remote/src`, `packages/heos-core/src` and the Kotlin module is RFC1918, link-local, multicast (239.255.255.250) or localhost. Exceptions are on an explicit allow-list (the Play badge link opens a browser, it is not a fetch).
2. **No analytics or crash SDKs:** the dependency list contains no known analytics or crash SDKs (a deny-list: firebase, sentry, bugsnag, amplitude, segment, mixpanel, posthog, datadog, appcenter).
3. **Permissions:** the merged permission allow-list from §2 is asserted from `app.json` and the module manifest.
4. **Policy text:** the policy page contains each claim string. Removing the claim text or breaking a predicate fails the test.

## 5. Tip jar and supporter perks

**Library:** `expo-iap`, verified against SDK 58 / RN 0.88 / Play Billing 8 in the plan's first task. The fallback is `react-native-iap`. Either way it sits behind `src/runtime/billing.ts`:

```ts
interface Billing {
  available(): Promise<boolean>;
  products(): Promise<{ id: TipId; price: string }[]>;
  owned(): Promise<{ id: TipId; state: 'owned' | 'pending' }[]>;
  buy(id: TipId): Promise<'owned' | 'pending' | 'cancelled' | 'failed'>;
}
type TipId = 'supporter_2' | 'supporter_5' | 'supporter_10';
```

**Products:** three one-time non-consumable products at €2, €5 and €10 (Play converts other currencies). They are created through the Play API with the service account. Prices are always Play's localised strings.

**Ownership:**
- Checked on launch and when Settings opens; the result is cached as `supporter` in DeviceMemory.
- Every owned purchase is acknowledged, both after `buy` and on restore, because Play refunds unacknowledged ones after 3 days.
- **Pending:** shows "Pending"; unlocks when it completes.
- **Refunded:** disappears from `owned()`, and the perks switch off on the next check. The chosen accent and font revert to the defaults.
- **Billing unavailable** (sideloaded, no Play): the card is hidden silently.

**UI:**
- Settings gets a "Support Zapbar" card: three tiles with Play prices, ✓ on owned tiers, and the line "Zapbar is free and stays free. Tips unlock a badge and extra colours, nothing else."
- **Any tier unlocks every perk.** The tiers differ only in the amount; €2 gets exactly what €10 gets. Supporters get:
  - **Badge:** a small lime heart next to the Z in Settings.
  - **Accent colours:** four alternates to lime, chosen in Settings. Each passes WCAG AA (4.5:1) for its on-accent text in both light and dark mode, enforced by a test.
  - **Font:** a picker with "Plus Jakarta Sans" and "Comic Neue — for the lulz". Comic Neue is SIL OFL, Regular and Bold, and is bundled. Comic Sans MS itself is Microsoft-licensed and cannot be bundled.
- Perks apply in the app only. Widgets and the notification stay brand lime and Plus Jakarta Sans.

**Theme plumbing:** `textStyle` and the accent become theme values (from `useTheme`) instead of static constants, so the font and accent switch at runtime. This is a mechanical sweep across about 12 UI files, covered by the existing render tests plus new tests that a supporter's choice changes the rendered font and accent.

**Privacy:** Play Billing is IPC to the Play Store app, not a network call from Zapbar, so the tripwire stays intact.

## 6. Release flow

1. `scripts/version.mjs 1.0.0`, `bundleRelease`, then `release-check`.
2. The owner does the manual first upload to internal (§2).
3. Products are created through the API, and license testers are added.
4. The owner and their partner install from Play and run `docs/release/checklist.md`:
   - fresh install and setup;
   - remote, widgets, volume keys and notification;
   - test purchase, perks and restore after reinstall;
   - billing hidden on the sideloaded APK.
5. Owner sign-off.
6. **Ask, then** promote to production with `scripts/play/promote.sh`.
7. Once Google approves it: site live (if not already), launch posts at the owner's go.

## 7. Launch kit

`docs/launch/`:
- `README.md`:
  - value proposition: "the remote HEOS forgot to build";
  - feature list;
  - target persona: HEOS soundbar and AVR owners frustrated by the official app, who switch inputs daily;
  - privacy stance;
  - links.
- `posts.md`: drafts for r/denon, r/hometheater, r/homeassistant and Tweakers (in Dutch). Each is honest about the hardware it was tested on (DHT-S716H) and asks for reports from other models.
- `review-reply.md`: a short, disclosed ("I made this") reply template for HEOS 1★ review threads. Never astroturfed.

Nothing is posted by Claude without an explicit per-post go.

**Name check:** before launch, an EUIPO class-9 search for "Zapbar" and Zap-prefixed marks (the parent spec's Zapier watch item). The result goes to the owner.

## 8. Testing

**Jest:**
- `Billing` fake covering available/unavailable, owned, pending→owned, cancelled, failed, refund revoke and acknowledge-on-restore.
- Settings card states.
- Accent and font pickers gated on supporter.
- Perks revert on refund.
- Accent contrast.
- Privacy claims (§4).
- `version.mjs` mapping.
- `release-check` against a fixture manifest.

**Site:** a test that every page has a title and description, that links resolve within the site, and that no `<script>` or external `src` appears.

**Device:** `docs/release/checklist.md` (§6.4), run by the owner on the S24+ from the internal track.

## 9. Out of scope

- iOS (gate in §1).
- Per-user accent and font on widgets and the notification.
- A demo soundbar (only if review demands it).
- Crash reporting or analytics of any kind.
- Staged rollout.
- Other HEOS models beyond the existing graceful degradation.
