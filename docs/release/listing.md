## Full description

Zapbar is the remote HEOS forgot to build: switch inputs in one tap, use your phone's volume keys, and control your soundbar from a widget. Designed for function, no bloat.

WHAT IT DOES
• Every input on one screen, with real device icons: TV, PS5, PS4, Xbox, Switch, Steam Deck, Apple TV, Chromecast, Fire TV and more
• Volume and mute with a smooth slider, plus your phone's volume keys while the soundbar plays
• Sound modes, dialogue enhancer, night mode and EQ
• Home-screen widgets, a media notification and a Quick Settings mute tile
• A floating volume slider you can open from anywhere
• Rename your soundbar and your inputs
• Light and dark mode

WHAT YOU NEED
A HEOS device on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. Zapbar is tested on the Denon DHT-S716H; other HEOS devices work for the basics and gain features as they are tested. Tell us your model at support@zapbar.app.

FREE
Zapbar is free and stays free. Tips unlock a supporter badge, extra accent colours, Comic Neue and some very silly perks. Nothing else, ever.

PRIVACY
Zapbar only talks to devices on your own network. No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads. Your settings stay on your phone. Tips are processed by Google Play.
