# Reply template for HEOS complaint threads

Use at most once per thread, only where someone complains about the HEOS app's remote features, and only from your own account. Always disclose.

## English
Same frustration here, so I made a free Android remote for HEOS soundbars: one tap per input, phone volume keys, widgets, no account. Tested on a DHT-S716H so far: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.zapbar.remote
