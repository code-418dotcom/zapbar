## r/denon

**Title:** I got fed up with the HEOS app, so I built a remote for my soundbar (free, Android)

**Body:**
Switching from the TV to the PS5 in the HEOS app takes me five taps and a spinner. So I built Zapbar: one screen with every input as a big button, volume on a slider, and your phone's volume keys control the soundbar while it plays — even on the lock screen. There are home-screen widgets, a notification with volume and inputs, and a Quick Settings mute tile.

It talks only to the soundbar on your Wi-Fi: no account, no cloud, no analytics.

Honest caveat: I've only tested it on my own Denon DHT-S716H. Other HEOS devices should work for inputs and volume; if you try it on something else, I'd love to hear what works and what doesn't.

Free on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.zapbar.remote — there's an optional tip jar that unlocks silly perks (yes, including Comic Neue), nothing else.

I'm the developer — happy to answer questions here.

## r/homeassistant

**Title:** Zapbar: a local-only Android remote for HEOS devices (no account, no cloud, no telemetry)

**Body:**
Not an integration, but it might interest people here: Zapbar is an Android remote for HEOS soundbars that talks to the device directly over the LAN (HEOS CLI on 1255 plus UPnP), with SSDP discovery. No account, no cloud, no analytics or crash reporting; the privacy policy's claims are enforced by tests in the build.

What you get: one-tap inputs, phone volume keys mapped to the bar, widgets, a notification, a QS mute tile, EQ.

Tested on a Denon DHT-S716H; other HEOS models should do the basics. Free, with an optional tip jar for cosmetic perks.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.zapbar.remote

I'm the developer — ask me anything about the protocol side.
