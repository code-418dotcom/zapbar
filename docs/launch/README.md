# Zapbar — launch kit

## Value proposition
The remote HEOS forgot to build: one-tap inputs, your phone's volume keys, widgets and EQ. No account, no cloud, no bloat.

## Features
- Every input on one screen, with real device icons (TV, PS5, PS4, Xbox, Switch, Steam Deck, Apple TV, Chromecast, Fire TV, Blu-ray)
- Volume and mute with a smooth slider, plus the phone's volume keys while the bar plays (also on a locked screen)
- Sound modes, dialogue enhancer, night mode, bass/treble/subwoofer EQ
- Home-screen widgets (4×2 and 4×1), a media notification, a Quick Settings mute tile, a floating volume slider
- Rename the soundbar (written to the bar) and the inputs
- Light and dark mode, brand colours
- Free; tips unlock a badge, accent colours and Comic Neue

## Target persona
**Primary:** owners of HEOS soundbars and AVRs (Denon, Marantz) who switch HDMI inputs daily — TV, console, decoder — and gave the official HEOS app one star because switching an input takes five taps and a wait.
**Secondary:** Home Assistant and homelab people who want a fast, local-only remote with no account and no cloud.

## Privacy
- Zapbar only talks to devices on your own network.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads.
- No account and nothing to sign in to.
- Settings stay on the phone.
- Tips are processed by Google Play.
Every claim is pinned to the code by `apps/remote/__tests__/privacy-claims.test.ts`.
