Privacy

Effective 2026-11-11.

The app

Zapbar only talks to devices on your own network. It finds your soundbar on your Wi-Fi and sends commands straight to it.

Zapbar has no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads.

There is no account and nothing to sign in to.

Your settings stay on your phone. Uninstalling Zapbar removes them.

Tips are processed by Google Play or the App Store; Zapbar never sees your payment details. The store's own privacy policy covers that purchase.

This website

zapbar.app counts visits with Magpie Metrics, our own analytics service, running on our own server in the Netherlands. It sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. It records the page you visited, the site that linked you here, and your browser, operating system, device type and country. We use this only to see how many people visit and where they come from; it is never shared or sold.
