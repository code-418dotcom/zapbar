Zapbar

The remote HEOS forgot to build.

Designed for function, no bloat. Switch inputs in one tap, set the volume, and control your soundbar from a home-screen widget. On Android and iPhone.

What it does

  • Every input on one screen, with real device icons: TV, PS5, Xbox, Switch, Apple TV and more
  • Volume and mute with a smooth slider, plus your phone's volume keys on Android
  • Sound modes, dialogue enhancer, night mode and EQ
  • Home-screen widgets for volume, mute and inputs, on Android and iPhone
  • On Android also a notification, a Quick Settings mute tile and a floating volume slider
  • Rename your soundbar and your inputs
  • No account, no cloud: it talks only to your soundbar on your Wi-Fi

Free

Zapbar is free and stays free. Tips unlock a badge, extra accent colours and Comic Neue, nothing else.
