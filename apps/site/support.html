What you need

A HEOS device on the same Wi-Fi as your phone. Zapbar is tested on the Denon DHT-S716H; other HEOS devices work for the basics and gain features as they are tested.

Questions

Zapbar doesn't find my soundbar
Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the soundbar, not a guest network. You can also type the soundbar's IP address on the setup screen.
On iPhone, Zapbar asks for Local Network access
Zapbar needs it to find and talk to your soundbar on your Wi-Fi. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Zapbar.
The volume keys stop working after a while on my Samsung (Android)
One UI puts apps to sleep. In Zapbar, open Settings → Remote → "Keep volume keys working when the app is closed" and allow Zapbar to run unrestricted.
I tipped and reinstalled; where are my perks?
Android: install Zapbar from Google Play with the same Google account and open Settings; your tip is restored automatically. iPhone: open Settings → Support Zapbar → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
